# Code of Conduct

Open AMPP follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be kind and welcoming, assume good intent, accept constructive feedback gracefully and focus on what is best for the community. Harassment, insults, personal or political attacks and publishing others' private information are not acceptable, in any language.

This applies to issues, pull requests, discussions and any other project space, and to anyone representing the project elsewhere.

## Reporting

If you experience or witness unacceptable behaviour, contact the maintainer, [@Jmzp](https://github.com/Jmzp), privately through the email shown on their GitHub profile. Reports are handled confidentially. Maintainers may remove comments, commits or contributions that break this code, and temporarily or permanently ban those responsible, following the Covenant's [enforcement guidelines](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/#enforcement-guidelines).
